# Victory and defeat

Our own rules; the pictures and speech at the end are the original's.

## Out of the game

An empire that has lost its last colony is out of the game: its fleets
are gone and the turn report says so.

## How a game ends

After each turn, for the player:

- **Defeat:** the player is out of the game, or another empire holds two
  thirds of all systems.
- **Victory by conquest:** every other empire is out of the game.
- **Victory by domination:** the player holds two thirds of all systems
  (every colony, and the home of every minor race that has not joined
  anyone). Not in a vendetta game.
- **Victory with allies:** in a team play game, the player is allied with
  every empire still in the game.

The player can also retire from the main menu.

## The end

The original's pictures and speech for the player's empire and the ending
play (its `<x>eog<kind>.scr` scripts), then the hall of fame. A click,
key or button skips to the hall of fame.

## Score

For each colony 100 and its people (millions); 25 for each technology
level; a twentieth of the credits; 3000 for conquest, 2000 for domination,
1500 for victory with allies. The total is multiplied by 0.6 (simple),
0.8, 1.0, 1.2 or 1.4 (impossible).

## Hall of fame

The ten best games, kept in `fame.txt` beside the settings, one line each:
score, empire and how the game ended, separated by `|`.
