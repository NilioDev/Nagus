# Random events

Our own rules, with the game's texts. Only when *random events* is on in
the game set-up.

Each turn each empire has a 4 in 100 chance of an event at one of its
colonies (or fleets), picked at random among:

| Event | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Plague | a tenth of the people die |
| Earthquakes | one or two structures are destroyed, a twentieth of the people die |
| Comet | a tenth of the people die |
| Immigration | a tenth more people, up to what the system can hold |
| Terrorists | one structure is destroyed |
| Hull virus | the ships of one fleet lose a quarter of their hull |

An event that cannot happen there (no structures, no fleet, too few
people) does not happen that turn. A colony always keeps at least one
million people. The player hears of the events that strike their empire
in the turn report.
